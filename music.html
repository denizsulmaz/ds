Sardaana

Latest Release · March 2026
0:00 Preview Clip 0:30

Behind the Track:
Sardaana is a cinematic orchestral opera piece created with Veronika Dyagileva, a Yakutian soprano from the Mariinsky Theatre, and Sakha artist Invent. Performed in the Yakutian language, the song celebrates the Sardaana, a native red lily that blooms with the return of sunlight in one of the world's coldest regions. Inspired by Sakha culture, it symbolizes joy, love for the land, and the connection between people and nature.

ABOUT / #composer

Composing systems like composing music.

It’s Deniz Sulmaz, aka “Who is this, DS?”. I create across music production, composition, and creative coding. My work moves between genres, ideas, and emotional states, shaping a discography that embraces "intentional chaos". Each release is an experiment in perception.

Music is a way of experiencing life through attentive listening. I express my emotions through sound rather than saying them outright, and I invite you to experience that world with me.
